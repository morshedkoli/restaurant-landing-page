Our Story

A Passion for
Authentic Flavour

Founded in 1998, The Grand Indian was born from a simple belief: that Indian cuisine deserves to be experienced in all its depth and complexity. Our founders brought recipes passed down through generations — from the clay kitchens of Punjab to the coastal spice markets of Kerala.

Every dish on our menu is a celebration of regional diversity. We source whole spices directly, grind them fresh each morning, and cook using traditional methods that cannot be rushed.
